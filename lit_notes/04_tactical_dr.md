## Deep notes on the most relevant papers

### 1. Tang, Wang, Zhang 2023 (A16): the closest prior art to direction (5)
- FACT: StatsBomb 360 freeze frames -> 10-channel 64x96 grids -> CNN autoencoder trained on 3 pretext tasks -> K-means inside each of 30 pitch zones -> 95 hand-named "situations" -> Situational xT. Code is public.
- FACT: k is chosen per zone "manually ... with the best interpretability" (range 2-10). There is no seed, bootstrap, or encoder-retraining stability check, and no projection-quality metric. The authors themselves concede that samples may not match their cluster description.
- SYNTHESIS: this is almost exactly "learned representation + clustering -> tactical states" on StatsBomb-style data. **Direction (5) as stated is already done.** It also gives a concrete, reproducible target for a reliability audit: "would these 95 situations survive a different seed, k, or encoder?"
- Feasibility: EPL 2021/22 360 data is not in the open repo (SYNTHESIS). A student could retrain their public code on open 360 competitions (Euro 2020, WC 2022, Euro 2024 per the open-data repo; verify the exact list before committing).

### 2. Jeon et al. 2025/26 "Stop misusing t-SNE and UMAP" (B11): framing for direction (6)
- FACT: 136 VA papers reviewed; t-SNE 75, UMAP 31; UMAP has the highest misuse-to-usage ratio; >40% of papers give no rationale. Misuse is concentrated in "global" tasks (cluster distance and density), and it persisted after the Distill 2016 and UMAP 2018/19 guidance.
- SYNTHESIS: soccer papers reproduce exactly this pattern. EventGPT (A12) reads inter-cluster adjacency off t-SNE ("wing-backs emerge in an intermediate space between defenders and wingers"), and Baron et al. (A10) read UMAP visually with no metric. This supplies the "motivating misuse" for a soccer case study, and it is a citable, concrete one.
- Caution: the arXiv comment says VIS 2026/TVCG, but another listing said "submitted to". Cite it as an arXiv preprint unless the TVCG DOI is confirmed.

### 3. Atzberger et al. VIS 2024 (B15): the methodological template (and the saturation threat)
- AUTHOR CLAIM: 42,817 layouts over embedding model x DR x hyperparameters x seeds, 10 metrics, with guidelines. Code and results are public.
- SYNTHESIS: this is a direct template for "large-scale sensitivity analysis of 2D maps of domain embeddings". Doing the same for soccer is a **domain transfer**, not a methods contribution. A course project can still defend it if the soccer-specific validation layer (proxies, see (iv)) is the novelty.

### 4. Bauer/Anzer/Shaw 2023 + Biermann et al. 2025 + Rothe et al. 2026 + Li & Link 2026 (A1-A4): phase labels
- FACT: every phase-of-play system found uses **proprietary DFL/TRACAB tracking**. Labels are expert-defined. Agreement is reported by Bauer (inter-labeler F1, e.g. 0.78 for mid-block) and Biermann (multi-annotator, about 80%). Li & Link explicitly have **no** inter-rater reliability (single annotator). Rothe et al. compare against a human annotator (apparently one).
- FACT (A5): even basic formation labels from three data vendors agree only 30% of the time (13/44).
- SYNTHESIS: "ground truth" tactical labels are both unavailable publicly and low-agreement. This argues *against* validating projection clusters with subjective tactical labels, and *for* using objective proxies plus measures that do not assume classes are clusters (Label-T&C, adjusted IVMs: B8, B10).

### 5. TacticAI (A9) and Fassmeyer et al. (A8): the "good practice" end of soccer
- FACT: both use t-SNE only illustratively, and they validate the *embedding* through a downstream task (a human-rated retrieval study; few-shot AUC). Neither tests whether the *2D picture* is faithful.
- SYNTHESIS: the soccer literature validates embeddings by downstream performance or does nothing, but never validates the projection itself. The gap for (6) is the projection layer.

## Adjudication

**(i) Is "use UMAP/t-SNE on soccer embeddings" already done?** **Yes, clearly (FACT).** UMAP: Baron et al. 2024 (StatsBomb open data), Lopes & Machado 2021. t-SNE: TacticAI 2024, EventGPT 2025/26, Fassmeyer 2021, the MLSA 2025 next-event paper. Clustering learned soccer representations into "situations/states": Tang et al. 2023, Rothe et al. 2026, Decroos 2018, the DEC playing-styles paper 2025. **Direction (5) on its own is not a gap.**

**(ii) Has anyone systematically evaluated projection or cluster reliability for SPORTS embeddings?** **I found no such study (SYNTHESIS, with a coverage caveat because Semantic Scholar was rate-limited).** Closest items:
- Deb et al. 2024: repeatable k-means (SeCo) on hand-crafted pass features, with no DR and no learned embeddings.
- Akhanli & Hennig 2023: calibrated validity indices on aggregate player stats.
- Lopes & Machado 2021: 4 distance metrics compared by eye.
- Tang et al. 2023: k chosen by interpretability.

None measures trustworthiness/continuity, steadiness/cohesiveness, Label-T&C, seed/hyperparameter sensitivity, or encoder-seed sensitivity for sports embeddings. **This is a real but narrow gap.** Before committing, run one targeted check: papers citing ZADU, Label-T&C, GhostUMAP, or Atzberger 2024 that mention sport, soccer, or trajectory.

**(iii) Is the general methodology saturated?** **Largely yes (SYNTHESIS).**
- Metrics: ZADU, Label-T&C, adjusted IVMs.
- Point-wise reliability: scDEED, LOO-map, GhostUMAP2.
- Ensembles: Nylund et al.
- Large-scale domain sensitivity studies: Atzberger 2024 for text; Chari & Pachter and Lause et al. for single-cell.
- Misuse surveys: Jeon 2025.

The contribution would therefore be a **domain application**. Possible soccer-specific twists (SPECULATION about novelty):
- (a) Separate three sources of instability: encoder training seed and pretext task, DR seed and hyperparameters, and clustering (HD space vs 2D). Atzberger varies embedding *models* but, as far as I can tell from the abstract, not encoder *training seeds*.
- (b) Test whether specific *tactical claims* survive, rather than only generic metrics. Examples: "counter-attack possessions form their own cluster", "wing-backs sit between defenders and wingers".
- (c) Use proxy labels that are *not inputs* to the encoder, to avoid circularity.

**(iv) Can tactical states be validated without subjective labels?** **Partially, yes (SYNTHESIS).** Objective public proxies:
- StatsBomb `play_pattern`. FACT: "From Counter" is rule-defined (possession starts with an open-play turnover outside the counter-attacking team's final third, is >= 75% direct, and travels >= 18 yards); other values include Regular Play, From Corner, From Free Kick, From Throw In, From Goal Kick, From Keeper, and Other.
- Possession outcome: shot, goal, turnover, or xG sum. This is the Decroos-style outcome ranking.
- Start and end pitch zone, team or opponent, `under_pressure`, game state (score, minute).
- Features derived from 360 freeze frames: defensive-line height, number of players behind the ball.

Caveats:
- (1) **Leakage/circularity.** If a proxy (such as x/y or event type) is an input, then "UMAP shows it" is trivial. Baron et al. fall into this.
- (2) **Classes are not clusters (B8, B10).** First test whether a proxy even forms clusters in the high-dimensional space, using adjusted IVMs, before blaming the projection. B16's precision/recall split does exactly this.
- (3) Proxies measure *consistency with known structure*, not tactical truth. Frame the result as reliability or faithfulness, not "discovering tactics".

## Feasibility notes (solo, ~40 h, strong ML, weak frontend, public data)

- **Recommendation (SYNTHESIS):** merge (5) into (6). Frame the RQ as: *"When 2D projections of learned soccer possession/situation embeddings are used to claim tactical structure, which claims survive changes in encoder seed, DR method/hyperparameters, and DR seed, and do the surviving structures agree with objective event-data proxies?"* Motivating cases: A10, A12, A16. Method template: B15. Metrics: B8-B10 via `zadu`. Point-wise stability: B14 or bootstrap Jaccard (B18).
- **Data:** StatsBomb open data (events plus 360 freeze frames for some tournaments). Optional check on real tracking: Bassek et al. 2025 (7 DFL matches, CC-BY). OpenSTARLab preprocessing can standardize providers, but it has **no** DR or cluster tooling, so it is only a loader (FACT from full text).
- **Budget sketch (SPECULATION):**
  - ~8 h: data plus a small encoder. Options: retrain Tang et al.'s public autoencoder on open 360 data, or a small transformer/GRU autoencoder over possession sequences. Use 3-5 training seeds.
  - ~6 h: DR grid (PCA, t-SNE, UMAP, optionally PaCMAP) x 3-5 hyperparameter values x 5 seeds.
  - ~6 h: metrics (T&C, MRRE, Steadiness/Cohesiveness, Label-T&C on proxies, adjusted IVM in high-dimensional space; ARI/Jaccard across runs).
  - ~10 h: visual interface in Plotly/Altair/Panel or Streamlit. Include small multiples across seeds, a point-wise stability overlay, linked pitch view of selected possessions, and a claim-checker panel. Avoid bespoke D3 given the weak frontend.
  - ~10 h: write-up.
- **Risks:**
  - (1) The headline "UMAP is unstable" is already known. The contribution must be the *soccer claim audit* and the *proxy-validity* layer.
  - (2) Open 360 data covers mostly national-team tournaments, so there are few matches per team. Use possession-level units (thousands per tournament) rather than team-level units.
  - (3) An encoder that is too weak makes everything look unstable. Report a simple non-learned baseline embedding (hand features) so the instability can be attributed to the right source.
